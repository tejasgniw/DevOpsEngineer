# Intact Interview – Recruiter Notes

* **AWS is the main priority**; GCP is secondary.
* Intact already has **2 GCP experts** on the team.
* **Terraform** is very important.
* **Python** is important.
* **GitHub Actions** is the main CI/CD platform.
* Be prepared to explain how I can **adapt my GitLab CI/CD experience to GitHub Actions**.

### Interview Format

* **One interview**, approximately **30 minutes–1 hour**.
* Both **soft skills and technical skills** will be evaluated.
* Soft skills are **very important**, not just technical knowledge.
* Expect **scenario-based / situational questions**.
* There will be a **DevOps person asking technical questions**.

### What They Are Looking For

* **Proactive**
* **Dynamic**
* **Motivated**
* Good **team integration / collaboration**
* Adaptable
* Comfortable working in a **small team**
* Able to **handle pressure**
* Understands **enterprise governance and security**

### Team

* Small DevOps team: approximately **5 DevOps engineers**.
* Approximately **10 people in the broader team**.

### Interview Flow

* Randstad recruiter will **introduce me and support during the interview**.
* Recruiter will stay and listen during the interview.
* Cynthia (Intact manager) will:

  * Introduce herself.
  * Explain the **project/team**.
  * Explain what they are working on.
* **Take notes while Cynthia presents.**
* Pay attention to what she emphasizes — this will show **what is important to her**.
* During my presentation, **connect my experience directly to what she presented**.
* After my presentation:

  * Cynthia will ask questions.
  * There will be a discussion / Q&A.

### How to Answer

* For scenario questions, use:

  * **Situation / Context**
  * **Task**
  * **Action**
  * **Result**
* When presenting an example, **start with context** so the interviewer understands the situation.
* Show specifically **what I did**, not just what the team did.

### Enterprise Experience

* Emphasize experience working in **large companies / enterprise environments**.
* Demonstrate understanding of:

  * **Governance**
  * **Security**
  * Structured processes
  * Enterprise standards
* Explain **how I worked within governance and security requirements**.
* Show that I am **adaptable** to enterprise processes.

### Proactivity

* Don't just explain what I was assigned.
* Show examples where I:

  * Identified a problem.
  * Took initiative.
  * Proposed/improved something.
  * Automated something.
  * Improved reliability/security/efficiency.

### Motivation

* They are looking for someone **motivated**.
* Be prepared to explain **why I am interested in Intact**.
* Know some information about **Intact and the project**.
* Demonstrate genuine interest in the role.

### Questions for Intact

* **Prepare 2–3 questions** to ask after my presentation.
* Asking questions is important because it shows that I am **interested and engaged**.
* Don't hesitate to ask questions.

### Contract

* Initial mandate: **6 months**.
* It is described as a **long-term project**.
* Current commitment is only 6 months, with potential continuation.

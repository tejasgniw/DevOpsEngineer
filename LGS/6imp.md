- Scaling up and scaling out

Scaling up (vertical scaling) means adding more power like CPU or RAM to a single server.

Scaling out (horizontal scaling) means adding more servers or instances to share the workload.

- Change management vs Configuration management

In DevOps, change management and configuration management are two sides of the same coin, but they focus on entirely different things.

Change management governs the process and approval of making a change to minimize risk. e.g. Request for Change (RFC), pull requests, and approval logs in Jira, Github/Gitlab

Configuration management maintains and tracks the actual state of your systems and code at any given moment. e.g. Ansible, Terraform, Puppet, Kubernetes ConfigMaps.

Job Title: DevOps Engineer

Location: Montreal, Quebec (Hybrid Mode)
Employment Type: Permanent Position

LGS, an IBM company, is a Canadian leader in IT consulting and business solutions. With over 40 years of experience, LGS supports major public and private organizations in their digital transformation. As an IBM subsidiary, we combine local Quebec expertise with the global resources and innovation of a leading technology company.

We are looking for a passionate and experienced DevOps Engineer to join our cloud infrastructure and automation team in Montreal.

The DevOps Engineer will be responsible for designing, implementing, and maintaining highly available and scalable cloud infrastructure on AWS. This professional will play a key role in automating deployment processes, managing infrastructure as code, and optimizing CI/CD pipelines for large-scale clients.

Key Responsibilities
Cloud Infrastructure and Automation

Design, deploy, and manage complex AWS cloud infrastructure (EC2, S3, RDS, Lambda, VPC, IAM, ECS/EKS, CloudFormation, etc.)

Develop and maintain Infrastructure as Code (IaC) using Terraform to ensure reproducibility and scalability

Create reusable Terraform modules and maintain code management best practices

Automate provisioning and configuration of cloud resources

Optimize cloud resource usage to reduce costs while maintaining performance

CI/CD and Automation

Design, build, and optimize robust CI/CD pipelines using Jenkins

Automate build, test, deployment, and rollback processes

Integrate automated testing practices into pipelines

Develop advanced automation scripts using Python and Boto3

Create tools and scripts to simplify daily operations

Implement deployment strategies (blue-green, canary, rolling updates)

Security and Compliance (DevSecOps)

Implement and maintain DevSecOps security practices

Implement secrets and credential management (AWS Secrets Manager, HashiCorp Vault)

Ensure compliance with security standards (PIPEDA, SOC2, ISO 27001)

Conduct regular infrastructure security audits

Configure and manage IAM policies based on the principle of least privilege

Monitoring and Performance

Implement monitoring and observability solutions (CloudWatch, Prometheus, Grafana, Datadog)

Configure proactive alerts and performance dashboards

Analyze performance metrics and identify optimization opportunities

Ensure high availability and system resilience in production

Manage incidents and participate in on-call rotations if required

Collaboration and Technical Leadership

Work closely with development, QA, and product management teams

Participate in Agile/Scrum ceremonies and planning meetings

Provide technical mentorship to junior team members

Conduct code reviews and promote best practices

Contribute to technical architecture decisions

Represent the DevOps team in client discussions

Required Qualifications
Experience and Education

Minimum 5 years of professional experience as a DevOps Engineer, SRE, or similar role

Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Computer Engineering, or equivalent experience

Proven experience managing large-scale cloud infrastructure projects

Experience working in Agile/Scrum environments
